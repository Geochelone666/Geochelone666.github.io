Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Bearmie Music Automation ("the App") accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

1. App purpose

The App is an owner-operated Windows workflow for preparing, uploading and monitoring music videos on the operator's own YouTube channel. It is not a public service that accepts uploads from visitors. This informational website has no registration, contact form, advertising tracking or embedded analytics.

2. Data accessed

After the operator authorizes access through Google's OAuth flow, the App may read the owned YouTube channel's identity, video identifiers, metadata, processing and privacy status, scheduled publishing status, and basic video statistics (such as views, likes and comments) within the granted permissions. It can submit the operator's selected video media and metadata to YouTube through the official YouTube Data API.

3. Data use

Google user data is used only to carry out the authorized operator's channel-management tasks: uploading and verifying videos, checking private and scheduled publications, preventing duplicate submissions, diagnosing errors and generating local operational reports. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, shared with data brokers, or used to train general-purpose AI systems.

4. Storage and protection

OAuth refresh credentials are stored locally on the operator's Windows device, protected with Windows user-scoped encryption and local file access controls. Video identifiers, upload receipts, status and permitted statistics are stored locally for workflow continuity and audit. OAuth credentials and private reports are never intentionally placed in this public website's source code or sent to the website.

5. Sharing

Data necessary to complete approved YouTube operations is sent through Google's official OAuth and YouTube API services. The App does not disclose Google user data to marketers or unrelated third parties. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process basic site request information according to its own policies.

6. Retention, revocation and deletion

Local credentials and activity logs are retained by the operator as needed to operate and reconcile the workflow. The operator may stop the App, revoke its Google account connection at Google Account — Third-party connections, and delete the local credentials and operational records. Deleting local records does not remove videos already submitted to YouTube; those remain subject to the channel's YouTube account settings.

7. Policy changes

When the App's actual data practices change, this page will be updated with a revised effective date. We do not use Google data for purposes that are not disclosed here.

8. Contact

For privacy and support questions: bearmie.soundscape@gmail.com.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs is governed by the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.
