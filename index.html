Bearmie Music Automation

Bearmie Music Automation is a desktop workflow used by the authorized operator of a music channel to prepare, quality-check, upload and monitor long-form instrumental music videos.

What this app does

  • Prepares videos from locally approved audio and visual assets.
  • Uses the official YouTube API, with the channel owner's OAuth consent, to upload videos and read video status and basic channel information.
  • Checks private videos and scheduled releases, recording receipts and basic statistics on the operator's own computer.
  • Uses safeguards against duplicate uploads and unintended public releases.

Access and privacy

This application is intended for the authorized channel operator and is not a public video-upload service. Access to the channel is granted through Google's OAuth flow; users never provide their Google password to the app. The operator can revoke access from their Google account at any time.

Read our Privacy Policy to understand which Google account data is processed, how it is stored, and how access can be revoked.
